Principal Investigator

Megan L. Smith, Ph.D.

Principal Investigator

Megan is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Biological Sciences at Mississippi State University. She was previously an NSF Postdoctoral Fellow in Matthew Hahn's lab at Indiana University. Megan received her Ph.D. from The Ohio State University in 2020, working in the Carstens Lab, and holds dual bachelor's degrees in Biology and English from the University of Mississippi (2015).
